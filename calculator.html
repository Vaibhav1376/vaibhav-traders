Standard Units Guide for Maharashtra Construction

What is 1 Brass?

In Maharashtra, Sand and Aggregate (Khadi) are traditionally measured in Brass. 1 Brass equals 100 Cubic Feet (approx 2.83 cubic meters), weighing approximately 4.2 to 4.8 Metric Tons depending on moisture content.

Standard Cement Bag

1 standard cement bag weighs 50 Kilograms with a dry volume of approximately 1.23 Cubic Feet (0.035 cubic meters). 28.8 bags are required for 1 cubic meter of solid cement volume.

Standard Red Brick Rule

For a standard 9-inch brick wall in cement mortar, count approximately 900 to 950 bricks per 100 square feet of wall surface area, including a recommended 5% margin for handling and cutting wastage.
